About Us

About Power Soccer & Power FC

A soccer coaching resource for Canadian players and coaches since 1996.

Our Story

Power Soccer was formed in 1996 to create a soccer coaching resource for the growing population of Canadian players and coaches. Since then, it has grown to become one of the most respected player development programs in Canada.

That growth is a direct result of our exceptional coaching staff, who run dynamic and enjoyable training programs that are completely focused on player development. Today we offer year-round soccer schools, summer camps, team training, private training and more, all in Toronto and the GTA.

What We Stand For

We Emphasize

Skill, tactical development and the maximization of game enjoyment, through a training process that emphasizes fair play and respect.

We Believe

In nurturing and developing the mental, physical and technical characteristics of the youth player, through a dynamic and creative soccer training model.

We Encourage

Players to use their imagination and creativity while playing. Our innovative skills program gives every player the opportunity for total involvement.

Our Mission

Players who are enjoying soccer and are comfortable to make mistakes without criticism will develop best.

At Power Soccer we focus on allowing players to express their individuality while giving them concise feedback on how they can improve their game. Players follow an age- and skill-specific program that fosters development and greater enjoyment of soccer.

Proper player development is a long-term project that produces incredible results. Players receive informative, positive feedback through detailed Player Profile reports and constant communication with their coaches.

How We Train

Three ideas shape every Power Soccer session.

01

Smart Soccer

Coaches capture players playing smart soccer and give them the confidence to find their own solutions in the game. We stress possession soccer and encourage players to think about what they do with and without the ball. Coaches offer options and priorities of play, so players understand the fundamental principles of the game.

02

‘All Ball’

All training is done with the ball. Continuous contact with the ball leads to a remarkable improvement in every player's ability, builds confidence, and is a proven principle in advanced soccer nations. Players learn creative fakes and turns, shielding skills, ball tricks and moves to deal with pressure in the game.

03

Game Sense

Sessions promote quick decision making, speed of reaction and a sharper sense of the game. We build good soccer habits from an early age, combining skill exercises with lots of game play so players enjoy every session.

Coaching Methodology

What you'll see at every Power Soccer session.

  • Enthusiastic, well organized and qualified coaches
  • Well planned sessions with a skill or tactical focus
  • The ball is used throughout the session
  • Coaches let players solve problems
  • Keep it simple, don't over-coach
  • Fitness is achieved through ball skills
  • Creative sessions that challenge players to reach their potential
  • Respect the player, parent, coach and official: fair play
  • Positive feedback from coaches creates a stimulating learning environment
