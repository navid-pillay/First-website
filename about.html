About Power Soccer & Power FC

Developing young players in Toronto since 1996.

Our Story

Power Soccer Inc. has been running soccer programs in Toronto since 1996. What started as a soccer school has grown into one of the most respected player development programs in Canada.

Today we offer year-round soccer schools, summer camps, private training, and adult clinics at locations across the city, giving players of all ages a place to learn, compete, and grow.

The Power FC Academy

Power FC is our academy team program, formed in 2005 to give players a competitive pathway and elite-level opportunities to advance their game.

Boys Academy

Teams from U8 through U18.

Girls Academy

Teams currently from U8 through U14.

Academy Season

Runs January to November. Players can join squads during the year after an assessment.
