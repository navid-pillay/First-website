About Power FC

The club behind the crest, and the people who make it run.

Our Story

Power FC is the soccer club of Power Soccer Inc. We started with a simple idea: give players a place to love the game, get better every week, and be part of a team that feels like family.

Today we bring together players, coaches, volunteers, and supporters who share that same passion for the sport.

Our Mission

To grow the game of soccer by giving every player the coaching, opportunities, and community they need to succeed on and off the field.

Respect

For teammates, opponents, officials, and the game itself.

Effort

We show up, work hard, and give our best every session.

Inclusion

Everyone is welcome at Power FC, whatever their background or ability.
