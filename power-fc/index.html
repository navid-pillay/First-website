[01]The Pathway

From Assessment
to the Crest

Every Power FC player follows the same pathway, built around long-term development.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Every player joins through a player evaluation, and squads can be joined during the year.

  2. 02

    Squad Placement

    Players join an age group squad in the boys academy (U8–U18) or the girls academy (U8–U14).

  3. 03

    Development

    Age and skill specific training, detailed Player Profile reports and constant communication with coaches.

  4. 04

    The Season

    A full academy year from January to November, playing for the Power FC crest.
