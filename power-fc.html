The Academy

Power FC is the academy team program of Power Soccer, built for players who want to compete and keep improving at a higher level.

Boys Academy

Teams from U8 through U18.

Girls Academy

The Power FC Girls Academy currently runs from U8 through U14.

Academy Season

The academy year runs from January to November.

Joining

Players can join squads during the year after an assessment.

How Our Players Develop

Academy players are coached the Power Soccer way, with a long-term focus on development.

01

Smart Soccer

Possession soccer and the confidence to find their own solutions in the game, with and without the ball.

02

Game Sense

Quick decision making and speed of reaction, sharpened through lots of game play.

03

Player Profiles

Detailed Player Profile reports and constant communication with coaches show every player how to improve.
