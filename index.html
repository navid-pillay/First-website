Our Programs

For close to three decades, Power Soccer has grown into one of the most respected player development programs in Canada.

Power FC Academy

Our competitive academy team program, giving players elite-level opportunities to advance their game.

Soccer Schools

Year-round skills sessions that build technique, confidence, and a love of the game.

Summer Camps

Fun, high-energy camps that keep players learning and playing all summer long.

Private Training

Focused, individual coaching tailored to each player's goals.
